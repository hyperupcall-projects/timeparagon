# Routines

**Routines** in the top bar are short, guided rituals for planning, tidying
up and reflecting, one step at a time. The screen opens on an overview of the
day's, week's and month's routines, each marked done or not, with the one
that suits the time of day marked **Suggested**. ◀ and ▶ move the overview to
another day, to plan tomorrow or reflect on a day that has passed.

| Routine | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| Daily plan | Start fresh · Choose tasks · Size it up · Timebox · Highlight |
| Daily refresh | Overview · Decide · Done |
| Daily reflection | Review · What's left · Reflect · Shut down |
| Weekly plan | Look back · Objectives · Spread it out · Focus |
| Weekly reflection | Review · Objectives · Reflect · Wrap up |
| Monthly plan | Look back · Objectives · Backlog · Focus |
| Monthly reflection | Review · Objectives · Reflect · Wrap up |

**‹ Routines** (or Escape) leaves a routine without losing your place: the
overview offers to **Continue** it. Steps can be revisited from the numbered
chips at the top, except in the daily refresh, which goes in order.

Everything written in a routine is saved as you type and synced between
devices (see [Routines](./data-model.md#routines) in the data model). Task
changes are made the normal way and show greyed out until Google confirms
them. The app sends one task change at a time, so choices made quickly wait
their turn; the footer counts what is still being saved, and you can carry
on in the meantime.

## Daily plan

1. **Start fresh**: what you finished yesterday, yesterday's highlight and
   what you meant to start with today (from yesterday's reflection), this
   week's objectives, and how many tasks are unfinished or in the
   inbox. **Run the daily refresh first** sorts those out, then comes back to
   the plan.
2. **Choose tasks**: the day's tasks, with **Tomorrow** and **Backlog** to take
   one off, and a box to add a new one for the day. Below are lists to pull work
   in from, each with **+ Today**: unfinished from earlier days, due within a
   week, later this week, triaged, the backlog's *next week or two*, and the
   inbox.
3. **Size it up**: one-press estimates and **P1** for each task, against the
   free time left in the day: your waking hours (from the sleep schedule, and
   from now for today) less meetings and other timed events. Time blocks count
   as free, since that is where tasks go. When the day is over capacity, each
   task also offers **Tomorrow**.
4. **Timebox**: the day view opens beside the panel. Drag a task onto it, or
   put it into one of the day's time blocks with a press.
5. **Highlight**: the one thing that would make the day a good day, typed or
   picked from the day's tasks (P1 first). The plan as text, with **Copy** for
   pasting into a chat or a note. **Finish planning** records the tasks and
   estimated time committed to, which the reflection compares against.

## Daily refresh

A pass over every task that needs a decision, one card at a time, in this
order:

| List | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| Unfinished | Not done, and past their deadline or planned for an earlier day, from Google Tasks or the calendar |
| Inbox | Waiting to be triaged |
| Today | Planned for today without a time |

A task in more than one list comes up once, in the first. For each one:

- **Do it on** today, tomorrow, a later day this week, or next week.
- **Park it** in a backlog horizon: next week or two, next month, next
  quarter, next year, someday or never.
- **Triage ✓** or **Leave in inbox** (inbox tasks), or **Keep for today**
  (today's tasks).
- **Done ✓**, **Ignore** or **Delete…** (which asks once more).
- **Skip** leaves it as it is.

A task past its deadline stays unfinished until the deadline is dealt with,
so the overview asks what happens to a passed deadline when such a task is
moved:
**move it with the task** (to the new day, or cleared when the task is
parked), **keep it**, or **clear it**.

**Back** returns to the previous card to change your mind; the new choice
replaces the old one if it has not been sent yet. **Edit details** (or the
title) opens the task's own editor beside the panel. While a card is up,
keys decide it: **T** today, **M** tomorrow, **B** someday, **D** done,
**S** or **→** skip, **←** back.

The last step counts what was decided and what is left, and the pass is
recorded for the day. A refresh can be run as often as you like.

## Daily reflection

1. **Review**: tasks done (and the share of the day's planned tasks), focus
   time recorded in time-block focus mode, time in meetings, and habits kept.
   What the morning's plan committed to and how much of it got done, and
   whether the day's highlight happened. The finished tasks, with a ☆ to pick
   highlights out, and focus time by project and by task.
2. **What's left**: the day's open tasks, each with **Tomorrow**, **Backlog**
   or **Done ✓**, or **Move all to tomorrow**.
3. **Reflect**: a rating from 1 (rough) to 5 (great), and four questions:
   what went well, what got in the way, what you learned, and the first
   thing to do tomorrow (which the next daily plan shows).
4. **Shut down**: what is planned for tomorrow, and the day as text to copy.

## Weekly and monthly plans

1. **Look back**: the last week (or month): tasks finished, its rating, focus
   and what you wanted to change, and its objectives, with **Carry over** for
   those not met.
2. **Objectives**: three to five outcomes for the period. A week shows the
   month's objectives beside it; a month shows your [life
   goals](./data-model.md#life-areas-goals-objectives-and-projects).
3. **Spread it out** (week): each day's tasks, planned time, free time and
   meetings, and lists to give a day to: the backlog's *next week or two*,
   tasks due this week, triaged and unfinished tasks. **Backlog** (month):
   tasks parked for next month, to bring closer or push back, those parked
   for next quarter, and what is due this month.
4. **Focus**: the period's focus in a line, and the plan as text to copy.

## Weekly and monthly reflections

1. **Review**: the same figures as the day's, over the period, with tasks
   done each day (or week) as columns (hover one for its focus and meeting
   time), the days' average rating, how many daily highlights were met, how
   many days were planned, refreshed and reflected on, and the highlights
   picked in the days' reflections.
2. **Objectives**: mark each one achieved or not, and carry those not met
   into the next period's plan.
3. **Reflect**: a rating, and the period's questions.
4. **Wrap up**: the period as text to copy.

Figures about meetings and time blocks need the period's calendar. The app
fetches it when a review opens, without writing anything to Google; until then
(or offline) the review says how many days it covers.
